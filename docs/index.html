Why open the phone tomorrow?

A daily ritualOne shared puzzle per day, like Wordle: short, finite, and something to talk about with friends.
A proven loopGeoGuessr-style guessing: look for clues, commit to a spot, see how close you were. Instantly clear, endlessly replayable.
Missing on SeekerOf 1,446 apps in the Solana dApp Store, none is a photo geo-guessing game. Neither is any public CLOCK IN repo we found.

  • Pinch the world map, tap your guess.

4

Seeker-native, on-chain, no backend

Mobile Wallet AdapterConnect Seed Vault, Phantom or Solflare. Everything is signed on the phone.
Seal your scoreA memo transaction atlas1|day|score|player|genesis|hints that references one board account. The leaderboard is that account's history.
Verified SeekerThe app finds the wallet's Seeker Genesis Token. Verified players get a badge — and the board keeps one entry per Genesis Token.
Can't spoof a SeekerMemo text is self-reported, so we trust it only as far as the chain backs it: fee payer must be the player, and the Genesis Token must really be in that wallet.
5

SKR as a game resource

Stuck on a photo? Burn 5 SKR to highlight its continent on the map.

  • BurnChecked on the SKR mint — tokens leave circulation. Nobody, including us, receives them.
  • Hints are written into your sealed score and shown on the leaderboard (💡2), so a clean run is worth more.
  • A small, repeatable reason to hold and spend SKR every day.
6

Honest about cheating

No spoilers in the appCredits name the place, so author and source link appear only after you guess. Full credits in the repo.
What we can't stopAny public photo can be reverse-image-searched, and answers ship in the APK. The board is for bragging rights, not prizes.
The real fixPhotos shot by Seeker owners: taken in-app, geotagged by the phone, never published anywhere else.

Roadmap

  • Seeker-shot photos. Owners snap places around them in-app; when a photo makes the daily five, players can tip its author in SKR.
  • Streaks & leagues. Daily streaks read from your sealed scores; a Seeker-only league with verified players.
  • Duels. Challenge a friend to the same five photos.
  • dApp Store release with weekly photo packs.
